## What You're Actually Comparing When You Compare VPS Hosts

When people search for a virtual private server hosting comparison, they're usually trying to answer one of three questions:

1. **Am I getting ripped off by my current host?**
2. **Which host gives me the most for the least?**
3. **What's the catch with the cheap ones?**

All fair questions. The answers depend on what you actually need — which most comparison articles gloss over in favor of a big table with checkmarks.

Here's what a useful virtual private server hosting comparison actually needs to look at:

- **Virtualization type** (KVM vs OpenVZ — KVM gives you true isolation and the ability to load custom kernels; OpenVZ is older and more restrictive)
- **RAM and CPU allocation** (some providers oversell nodes aggressively, so 2 GB RAM on paper becomes 1.4 GB in practice)
- **Storage type** (SSD vs HDD vs NVMe — speeds vary dramatically)
- **Bandwidth and port speed** (a 1 Gbps port is very different from a 100 Mbps port)
- **Network location** (West Coast for Asia-Pacific traffic, East Coast for European traffic)
- **Actual uptime** (not the "99.9% SLA" marketing copy, but what users report)
- **Support responsiveness** (does a ticket get answered in hours or days?)

Keep those in mind as we go.
